# Social Link Previews (Open Graph)

When you paste your website link into WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X, or Facebook, a preview card usually appears with a title, a short description, and an image. If that card looks wrong, this article explains why and how to ask Kite to fix it.

:::note What is Open Graph?
**Open Graph** is a small set of hidden tags on each page that tell social apps what title, description, and image to show in the preview card. **Twitter cards** are the same idea for X. You do not see these tags on the page; they only appear when your link is shared.
:::

## Why your preview looks wrong

- **The preview says "Prototype."** New Kite sites start with the title "Prototype" and an empty description until you set them. Ask Kite to set the title and description for the page.
- **The wrong image appears (or no image).** No preview image has been set for that page, so the social app picks something on its own, or nothing.
- **The old preview still shows after you fixed it.** Social platforms cache previews and may keep showing the old card for a while.

## How to ask Kite to set your social preview

1. Open your site in Kite (or ask in Slack).
2. Describe what you want for the page, for example: "Set the social link preview for the Home page. Title: Northstar Scheduling. Description: Scheduling software for multi-location clinics. Book a demo in two minutes. Use our logo as the preview image."
3. Publish your changes. See [How to publish](/publishing/how-to-publish).

## Check the result

Open the **Discoverability** tab on your site's Analytics page to see how your pages present to search engines and social apps. See [Site analytics](/forms-integrations/analytics).

:::tip Choose a good preview image
A clear, landscape image (your logo on a clean background or a strong photo of your product) reads best in preview cards. See [Images & logos](/building/images-and-logos).
:::

## Troubleshooting

- **The preview still shows the old info after I fixed it.** Confirm the change is live on your page, then re-share the link. Most platforms offer a "post inspector" or "sharing debugger" that forces them to re-fetch your page.
- **It still says "Prototype."** Ask Kite to set the title and description for that specific page, then publish.
- **The image is missing or cropped oddly.** Ask Kite to set a dedicated landscape preview image for the page.

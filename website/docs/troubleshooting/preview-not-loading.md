# Preview Won't Start

The preview pane is where you see your site as you build it. When it will not start, it is usually a temporary glitch, not a problem with your website. The fixes below clear it up in most cases.

## Symptoms

- The preview shows **Preview couldn't start** with a **Retry** button.
- The preview is blank or white.
- The preview does not reflect your latest change.

## Cause

Almost always a temporary loading problem: a slow connection, a momentary server hiccup, a browser extension getting in the way, or a stale browser session. Your site and its data are stored separately and are safe.

:::info Your site is safe
A preview that will not start does **not** mean you have lost your work. Your project, content, and versions are stored separately from the preview window.
:::

## Fixes to try in order

1. **Click Retry.** If the preview shows the Retry button, use it. This restarts the preview and fixes most cases.
2. **Refresh the page.** Reload the browser tab and give the preview a moment to rebuild.
3. **Wait a minute, then retry.** If a server was briefly busy, a short wait often clears it.
4. **Reopen the project.** Go back to your websites list and open the project again.
5. **Try a different browser, or disable extensions.** Ad blockers and privacy extensions can block parts of the preview. A private window usually disables them.
6. **Check your internet connection.**
7. **Contact support if it persists.** Open **Chat with us** and include your site URL, what you tried, and a screenshot.

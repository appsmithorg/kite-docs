# Get Started

Welcome to Kite. Kite is your AI marketer in Slack. It researches your business, builds a growth strategy, and ships the work that brings you new customers. This section takes you from adding Kite to your workspace to the first work it ships for you.

## In this section

- **[Add Kite to Slack](/get-started/add-kite-to-slack)**: sign in, connect Kite to your workspace, and invite it to a channel.
- **[How Kite works](/get-started/how-kite-works)**: what Kite does on its own, what it does when asked, and where credits fit in.
- **[Quickstart: From adding Kite to your first shipped work](/get-started/quickstart)**: the first conversation, the first draft, the first publish.

## Where to go next

Once Kite is in your channel and has shipped its first piece of work, most teams head to one of these:

- **[Approvals and autonomy](/slack/approvals)**: how Kite decides what to do on its own and how you give the OK.
- **[What Kite can do](/capabilities/)**: the kinds of work to ask for.
- **[Website Building](/website-building)**: build, edit, and publish the website Kite hosts for you, and connect your own domain.

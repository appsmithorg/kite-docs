# Get Started

Welcome to Kite. Kite is your AI marketer in Slack. It researches your business, builds a growth strategy, and ships the work that brings you new customers. This section takes you from adding Kite to your workspace to approving the first piece of work it ships for you.

## In this section

- **[Add Kite to Slack](/get-started/add-kite-to-slack)**: connect Kite to your workspace and invite it to a channel.
- **[How Kite works](/get-started/how-kite-works)**: the loop Kite runs, how approvals protect you, and where credits fit in.
- **[Quickstart: Your first week with Kite](/get-started/quickstart)**: a day-by-day look at the first week, from discovery to results.

Once Kite is in your workspace and has posted its first growth plan, most teams head to one of these:

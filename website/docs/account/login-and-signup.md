# Login & Sign Up

Most teams start with Kite by adding it to Slack. The browser workspace is where you sign in to manage billing, open the website editor, and review your account. This guide covers both, plus what to do when a login or email step does not go as planned.

## Start by adding Kite to Slack

1. Go to [kite.ai](https://kite.ai) and click **Add me to Slack**.
2. Choose your workspace and allow the permissions Slack shows you.
3. Invite Kite to the channel where you want it to work.

Kite begins its discovery research right away. See [Add Kite to Slack](/get-started/add-kite-to-slack) for the full walkthrough.

## Sign in to the browser workspace

1. Go to [kite.ai](https://kite.ai) and select **Login**, which takes you to `auth.kite.ai`.
2. Sign in with the method you used when you set up Kite. <!-- TODO: verify the sign-in methods offered (email and password, Slack, other providers) -->
3. Once signed in, you can open your site in the editor, view **Billing**, and manage your account.

:::tip Use the same identity every time
Always sign in with the exact email address or account you used when you set up Kite. A different email (even a small typo or a different provider) looks like a brand-new account.
:::

## Troubleshooting

### Verification link failing

If you receive a verification email and the link does not work or says it is expired:

- **Try again from the same browser and tab** you used to sign up. Opening the link in a different browser or device can cause it to fail.
- **Do not sign up again.** Repeated sign-ups can create duplicate accounts under the same email and make things harder to fix.
- If it still will not verify, **contact support** and let them know the email you used so they can help confirm your account.

### Password-reset email not arriving

If you requested a password reset and the email has not shown up:

- **Check your spam or junk folder.** Automated emails sometimes land there.
- **Wait a few minutes.** Delivery can take a little time. Avoid requesting it over and over.
- **Make sure you entered the correct email,** the one you actually used to set up Kite.
- If nothing arrives after several minutes and a spam check, contact support.

:::note
If reset emails (or other Kite emails) consistently do not reach you, your mail provider may be filtering them. See [Email sending troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/email-sending).
:::

### "Session has already ended" or login errors when you return

If you come back later and see a "session has already ended" message or another login error:

- **Start the login again from kite.ai** rather than reloading an old page.
- **Close any stale tabs** left open from a previous session. They can hold an expired session and cause errors.
- Try signing in fresh in a single, new tab.

### Kite is in Slack but you cannot find the browser login

The Slack install and the browser workspace are two doors to the same account. Go to [kite.ai](https://kite.ai), select **Login**, and sign in with the identity used during the Slack install. If you are not sure which identity that was, contact support with your Slack workspace name.

### Still cannot get in?

If none of the above works, contact support and include the email address on your account and your Slack workspace name so the team can locate it.

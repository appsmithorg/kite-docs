# Account & Settings

Everything about your Kite account lives here: signing in, who can do what on your team, getting a copy of your website, and deleting your account.

## What you can do here

- **Sign in** with Google or an email one-time code, and link your Slack identity to your Kite team.
- **Understand roles.** Owners manage billing; admins manage the team; members use Kite and the editor.
- **Export your site's code** as a ZIP from your site's Settings, on any plan.
- **Request account deletion** through support, after canceling any subscription.

:::tip Account versus subscription
Deleting your account and canceling your subscription are two different things. Canceling stops billing and keeps your account. Deleting removes your account, your personal teams, and their websites. See [Manage Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription) if you only want to stop paying.
:::

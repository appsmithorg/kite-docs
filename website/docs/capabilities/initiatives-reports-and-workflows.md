# Initiatives, Reports, and Workflows

Beyond individual tasks, Kite organises its work into initiatives, delivers longer results as hosted reports and dashboards, runs workflows on a schedule or a trigger, and can be extended with custom agents and skills. This page introduces each.

## Initiatives

An initiative is a goal with tasks under it. Kite proposes initiatives in Slack with an **Approve** button; click it and Kite breaks the initiative into tasks and starts. You can also create and track initiatives on the initiatives board in your browser workspace. Kite's hourly scan of your team's Slack messages is matched against active initiatives, which is how it finds the next move and posts a digest. <!-- TODO: verify the initiatives board's location and labels in the UI -->

## Workflows

A workflow is a piece of work Kite runs on its own, either on a schedule (a weekly conversion check, a monthly competitor sweep) or when a webhook fires (a release goes out, a form is submitted). Workflows are how you turn a one-off request into something that keeps happening. <!-- TODO: verify how workflows are created and where their run history appears -->

# What Kite Can Do

Kite is your AI marketer in Slack. It researches your business, builds a growth strategy, and ships the work that brings you new customers. This section describes the kinds of work Kite finds and ships, so your team knows what to expect and what to ask for.

Every capability below follows the same rule: Kite proposes the work in Slack, your team approves it, and only then does Kite ship it. See [How approvals work](/slack/approvals).

## In this section

- [Research and growth plans](/capabilities/research-and-growth-plan): Kite studies your product, customers, competitors, and what AI answer engines say about you, then proposes a growth strategy.
- [Content, outreach, and channels](/capabilities/content-and-outreach): Kite finds the conversations your prospects are already having on Reddit, social media, and email, and drafts your part in them.
- [Product marketing and launches](/capabilities/product-marketing): Kite prepares the product page, announcement, walkthrough, and email campaign while the code is still in review.
- [Conversion fixes](/capabilities/conversion-fixes): Kite finds pages with traffic that is not converting and proposes edits with previews.
- [Website building](/capabilities/website-building): Kite builds, hosts, and updates your website, and you can edit it yourself in the browser editor.
- [Free tools](/capabilities/free-tools): Growth Grader, LinkedIn to Website, and Website Makeover.

## What Kite is not

Kite is not a replacement for a marketing hire. It gives a founder or a lean team marketing capacity before there is a marketer on staff, and it works alongside that person once there is. It also never acts without approval: research and planning happen on their own, but nothing reaches customers until your team says so.

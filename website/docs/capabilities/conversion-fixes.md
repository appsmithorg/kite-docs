# Conversion Work

Traffic you already have is the cheapest growth available. Ask Kite why a page is not converting and it audits the page, finds the gap, builds the fix as a draft, and can test it against the original.

## What to ask for

- "Why isn't the pricing page converting?" Kite reviews the page, the analytics, and the path visitors take, then proposes a specific change with a preview.
- "Audit the site for conversion problems." Kite checks every page for missing calls to action, weak headlines, slow load times, and pages that rank but do not answer the searcher's question.
- "Run an experiment on the homepage headline." Kite sets up an A/B experiment that shows the original to some visitors and the new version to others. A person on your team starts it, Kite reports how it's going, and you keep the version that wins. See [A/B experiments](/capabilities/ab-experiments).

## Scheduling a recurring check

If you want this to happen without asking, set up a workflow that runs a conversion check every week and posts what it finds. See [Initiatives, reports, and workflows](/capabilities/initiatives-reports-and-workflows).

## Where the data comes from

Every Kite-hosted site has built-in analytics: pageviews, top pages, where visitors came from, and devices on paid plans, plus funnels when you ask Kite. Form submissions are in **Growth**, under **Leads**. **Site health** checks your site after every publish and has a **Have Kite fix this** button for each problem it finds. See [Site analytics](/forms-integrations/analytics) and [SEO & discoverability](/seo/).

## Making the change yourself

Every conversion fix is a website change, so you can also make it by hand in the browser editor: describe the edit in chat or click the element to change it, preview, and publish. See [Editing your site](/building/editing-your-site).

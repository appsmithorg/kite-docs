# Product Marketing and Launches

Tell Kite what is shipping and when, and it gets the launch ready before the code is.

## How a launch runs

1. **Tell Kite about the release** in a thread: what changed, who it is for, and when it lands. Paste the pull request description or the release notes if you have them.
2. **Kite prepares the assets** and posts them in the thread:
   - A product or feature page, written in language search engines can find, built as a draft on your site.
   - An announcement, ready for your blog and your connected social networks.
   - An email campaign, ready to send.
3. **You read it once.** Reply with changes, or with "ship it."
4. **Kite publishes** the page, sends the email, and posts the announcement to the networks you have connected.

Your team merges the pull request. Kite launches the release.

## Keeping launches on a rhythm

If you ship on a schedule, set up a workflow so Kite runs the launch checklist each time, or trigger it from a webhook when a release goes out. See [Initiatives, reports, and workflows](/capabilities/initiatives-reports-and-workflows).

## Why this matters for lean teams

On most small teams, launch marketing happens after the engineering work, if it happens at all. With Kite, shipping a feature and telling customers about it happen in the same week.

# Product Marketing and Launches

Kite gets your launch ready before the code is. When it is connected to GitHub, Kite watches pull requests and merges, and it starts preparing launch material while the change is still in review. By the time the feature ships, the marketing is waiting in Slack for one read and an approval.

## How a launch runs

1. **A pull request opens.** Kite notices the change and works out what it means for customers.
2. **Kite prepares the assets** and posts them in your `#product-marketing` channel:
   - A product page, written in language search engines can find.
   - An announcement draft.
   - A walkthrough, scheduled on the calendar.
   - An email campaign, ready to send.
3. **Your team reviews.** Read it once, adjust anything that needs it, and approve.
4. **Kite ships.** It publishes the page, sends the email, and posts the announcement after approval. See [How approvals work](/slack/approvals).

## Integrations Kite uses for launches

- **GitHub**, to watch commits, pull requests, and merges.
- **Resend**, to send broadcast email campaigns.

## Why this matters for lean teams

On most small teams, launch marketing happens after the engineering work, if it happens at all. Kite runs it in the background while the team is doing everything else, so shipping a feature and telling customers about it happen in the same week.

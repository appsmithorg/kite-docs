# Website Building

Your website is where Kite's work often starts. Kite builds it, hosts it, and keeps it updated, handling hosting, security, and updates so your team can focus on the business. Site changes follow the same rule as everything else Kite does: nothing goes live without your approval.

## How website work happens

There are two ways site work gets done, and most teams use both:

- **Kite proposes changes in Slack.** As part of its growth plan, Kite drafts new pages (for example, a comparison page) and fixes to existing ones, posts a preview, and publishes after your team approves. See [Conversion fixes](/capabilities/conversion-fixes).
- **You edit in the browser editor.** Open your site, describe a change in chat or click an element to change it directly, preview the result, and click **Publish**. See [Building & editing your site](/building/).

## What Kite handles for you

- **Building.** Kite writes the pages, layout, copy, and styling from a description of what you need.
- **Hosting.** Every published site gets a free `your-site.kite.space` address, and you can connect your own domain. See [Custom domains](/custom-domains/).
- **Search groundwork.** Kite generates page titles, a sitemap, and a robots file, and you can ask it to set titles, descriptions, and social previews. See [SEO & discoverability](/seo/).
- **Updates.** Ask for changes in Slack or the editor and Kite makes them.

## Starting a new site

If you do not have a site yet, describe what you need and Kite builds a first version. You can also turn a LinkedIn profile into a website in a few minutes with the free LinkedIn to Website tool. See [Free tools](/capabilities/free-tools).

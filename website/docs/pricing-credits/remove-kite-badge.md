# Remove the "Built with Kite" Badge

By default, sites built with Kite show a small "Built with Kite" badge. Removing it is a paid-plan benefit. This page explains who can remove the badge and how to do it.

## What the badge is

The badge is a small "Built with Kite" mark that appears on your published site. It helps spread the word about Kite and shows up automatically on free sites. <!-- TODO: verify badge placement and wording -->

## Who can remove the badge

Removing the badge requires a paid plan. <!-- TODO: verify which plan or plans include badge removal --> If you're on the free option, you'll need to upgrade first. See [Plans & Free Trial](/pricing-credits/plans-and-trial).

## How to remove the badge

Once you're on an eligible paid plan:

1. Make sure your plan is active. Check **Billing** (`/billing`) if you're not sure. See [Manage, Cancel, or Downgrade Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).
2. Find the badge or branding setting in your site settings and turn the badge **off**. <!-- TODO: verify exact location and label of the badge toggle -->
3. **Re-publish your site** so the change goes live. See [How to Publish](/publishing/how-to-publish).

:::info
The badge may stay visible on your live site until you re-publish. Publishing again pushes the updated, badge-free version out to your visitors.
:::

## Troubleshooting

**I upgraded but the badge is still there.**
Re-publish your site first — changes (including badge removal) usually go live only after you publish again. See [How to Publish](/publishing/how-to-publish).

**I re-published and it's still showing.**
Your browser may be showing a cached version. Try a hard refresh or check in a private/incognito window. See [Changes Not Showing](/publishing/changes-not-showing).

**It still won't go away after upgrading and re-publishing.**
Contact support and let them know you're on a paid plan but the badge remains. They can check your account.

**The badge came back after it was gone.**
This can happen if your paid plan ended or you downgraded. See [Manage, Cancel, or Downgrade Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).

# Remove the "Built with Kite" Badge

Sites built with Kite may show a small "Built with Kite" badge. This page explains who can remove the badge and how to do it.

## What the badge is

The badge is a small "Built with Kite" mark that appears on your published site. It helps spread the word about Kite. <!-- TODO: verify badge placement and wording -->

## How to remove the badge

1. Make sure your plan is active. Check **Billing** if you are not sure. See [Manage, Cancel, or Downgrade Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).
2. Find the badge or branding setting in your site settings and turn the badge **off**. <!-- TODO: verify exact location and label of the badge toggle -->
3. **Re-publish your site** so the change goes live. See [How to Publish](/publishing/how-to-publish).

:::info
The badge may stay visible on your live site until you re-publish. Publishing again pushes the updated, badge-free version out to your visitors.
:::

## Troubleshooting

**I upgraded but the badge is still there.**
Re-publish your site first. Changes (including badge removal) usually go live only after you publish again. See [How to Publish](/publishing/how-to-publish).

**I re-published and it is still showing.**
Your browser may be showing a cached version. Try a hard refresh or check in a private window. See [Changes Not Showing](/publishing/changes-not-showing).

**It still will not go away after upgrading and re-publishing.**
Contact support and let them know which plan you are on and that the badge remains. They can check your account.

**The badge came back after it was gone.**
This can happen if your paid plan ended or you downgraded. See [Manage, Cancel, or Downgrade Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).

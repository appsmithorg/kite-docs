# Remove the "Built with Kite" Badge

Sites on the Free plan show a small "Built with Kite" badge. Removing it is a Growth and Business feature. This page explains how.

## What the badge is

The badge is a small "Built with Kite" mark on your published site. It helps spread the word about Kite.

## How to remove the badge

1. Make sure your team is on Growth or Business. Check **Billing** if you are not sure. See [Plans & Pricing](/pricing-credits/plans-and-trial).
2. Open your site in the browser workspace and go to **Settings**.
3. Turn off **Show Kite badge on published website**.
4. **Publish** your site again so the change goes live. See [How to Publish](/publishing/how-to-publish).

:::info
The badge stays visible on your live site until you publish again.
:::

## If your plan lapses

The badge returns on your published sites when a team moves to the Free plan, including when the 5-day trial ends without an upgrade. Upgrade again and turn it off as above.

## Troubleshooting

**I upgraded but the badge is still there.**
Turn the setting off in your site's Settings, then publish again.

**I republished and it is still showing.**
Your browser may be showing a cached version. Try a hard refresh or a private window. See [Changes Not Showing](/publishing/changes-not-showing).

**The badge came back after it was gone.**
Your team has moved to the Free plan. See [Manage, Cancel, or Downgrade Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).

# Buy More Credits

If you have used your monthly credits and still have work to do, you can buy more without changing your plan. Kite lets you top up at any time without waiting for your billing month to reset.

## Monthly credits and top-up credits

- **Monthly credits** come with the Growth plan ($100 in credits each month) and reset each billing month.
- **Top-up credits** are extra usage you purchase on demand when you need more.

## Common questions

**Will buying credits change my plan?**
No. Top-ups add usage to your existing account without changing your plan or billing cycle.

**Where do I see how many credits I have left?**
Check your Billing page, which also lists the cost of each conversation and task. See [Manage, Cancel, or Downgrade Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).

**I bought credits but they are not showing up.**
Refresh the page first. If they still do not appear after a few minutes, contact support.

# Plans, Pricing & Credits

Kite combines two simple ideas: a **plan** that unlocks features (like custom domains and badge removal), and **credits** that power the AI as it builds and edits your site. This section explains how both work so there are no surprises on your bill.

## How Kite pricing works

Kite pricing has two parts that work together:

- **Plan tiers** — Your plan sets what your account can do. There's a free option to get started, plus paid plans that unlock features like connecting your own domain and removing the "Built with Kite" badge. <!-- TODO: verify with product -->
- **Credits (the AI token model)** — Every time the AI builds or edits your site, it uses credits. Bigger or more complex changes use more. Your plan includes a monthly credit allowance, and you can buy top-up credits if you need more.

:::tip
Think of your **plan** as your toolbox and your **credits** as the fuel. You need both to keep building.
:::

## Common questions

**Do I need a paid plan to try Kite?**
No. You can start building on the free option and upgrade when you're ready. See [Plans & Free Trial](/pricing-credits/plans-and-trial).

**What's the difference between a plan and credits?**
Your plan unlocks features; credits power the AI. You can run out of credits while still keeping your plan, and you can buy more credits without changing plans.

**Where do I change my plan or payment method?**
In your billing settings. See [Manage, Cancel, or Downgrade Your Subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).

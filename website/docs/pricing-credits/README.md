# Plans, Pricing & Credits

Kite pricing has two parts: a **plan** (Free, Growth, or Business) and **credits**, which every conversation with Kite and every task it runs draws from. This section explains how both work so there are no surprises on your bill. The authoritative source for current prices is always [kite.ai/pricing](https://kite.ai/pricing).

## How Kite pricing works

- **Plans.** Free includes the complete product, the Slack integration, and an allowance of usage. Growth is $100 per month and includes $100 in credits each month. Business has custom usage limits and hands-on help from the Kite team. Every plan covers your whole team. See [Plans & Pricing](/pricing-credits/plans-and-trial).
- **Credits.** Research, drafting, outreach, site edits, and retries all use credits. Bigger or more complex work uses more. Your Billing page shows the cost of each conversation and task, and you can top up at any time without waiting for your billing month to reset.

:::tip
Think of your **plan** as the size of your monthly budget and your **credits** as the budget itself. Kite spends credits on the work you approve and the conversations you have with it.
:::

## Common questions

**Do I need a credit card to start?** No. The Free plan has no subscription and no card requirement.

**Does my whole team need separate accounts?** No. One plan covers as many people as you like. See [Team access](/slack/team-access).

**Where do I see what I have spent?** Open the Billing page in your browser workspace. It lists the cost of each conversation and task. See [Manage your subscription](/pricing-credits/manage-subscription).

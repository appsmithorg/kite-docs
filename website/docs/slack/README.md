# Working with Kite in Slack

Kite is your AI marketer in Slack. Slack is where Kite brings your team its research, its recommendations, and ready-to-review work, and it is where your team approves that work before anything goes live. This section explains the day-to-day experience.

## The basic rhythm

1. Kite researches and finds something worth doing.
2. Kite posts a recommendation in your channel, with the evidence behind it and a draft when one is ready.
3. Someone on your team approves it, asks for changes, or declines.
4. Kite ships the approved work and reports back on how it performed.

You do not need to give Kite a task list. It finds the work and brings it to you. You can also ask Kite for specific work at any time. See [Asking Kite to do something](/slack/asking-kite).

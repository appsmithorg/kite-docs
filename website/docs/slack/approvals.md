# How Approvals Work

The approval loop is the core rule of working with Kite: **nothing goes live without your approval.** Kite researches, plans, and drafts on its own, but it ships only the work a member of your team has approved. This page explains how that works in practice.

## What needs approval

Anything that reaches the outside world needs approval before Kite ships it:

- Pages and page changes on your website.
- Replies and posts to conversations on Reddit, social media, or email.
- Email campaigns and announcements.
- Launch assets such as product pages and walkthrough invitations.

Research summaries, growth plans, and reports do not ship anywhere, so they do not need approval to exist. You still review them, because they shape the work Kite proposes next.

## How a draft reaches you

1. Kite posts the draft in your channel, with a short explanation of why it is proposing the work and the evidence behind it.
2. Where it makes sense, Kite includes a preview (for example, a preview of a page change) so you can see the result before it is live.
3. Kite waits. It does not publish, post, or send anything until someone on your team responds.

## Your three answers

**Approve.** Kite ships the work as drafted and reports back on how it performs.

**Ask for changes.** Reply in the thread with what to change. Be specific: name the section, the sentence, or the tone. Kite revises the draft and posts it again for approval.

**Decline.** Kite drops the work. If you tell it why, it uses that to improve what it proposes next.

Anyone on your team who is in the channel can approve work. One plan covers your whole team, so you do not need to buy a seat for each approver. See [Team access](/slack/team-access). <!-- TODO: verify whether approval rights can be restricted to specific people -->

## Tips

- **Decide quickly on small things.** A reply or a one-line page tweak is safe to approve fast. Save your careful review for pages, campaigns, and anything public-facing at scale.
- **Give reasons when you decline.** "Too salesy for this subreddit" teaches Kite more than a plain no.
- **Approve in the thread.** Keep the decision attached to the draft so the whole team can see what was agreed.

## Troubleshooting

- **Kite is not shipping anything.** Check for drafts waiting for approval in the channel. Kite does not ship until someone answers.
- **Something went live that you did not expect.** Check the thread for the approval. If you cannot find one, contact Kite support with the channel name and a link to the post.
- **You want to undo a shipped site change.** See [Undo changes & version history](/troubleshooting/version-history).

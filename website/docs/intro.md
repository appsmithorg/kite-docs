# Welcome to Kite

Kite is your AI marketer in Slack. It researches your business, builds a growth strategy, and ships the work that brings you new customers.

Most AIs wait for instructions. Kite finds what needs doing and does it.

This help center explains how to add Kite to your Slack workspace, how to work with it day to day, what it can do for your growth, and how to manage your plan and account.

## How Kite works with your team

Kite lives in the Slack channels you invite it to. Ask for work in a thread and Kite researches, drafts, builds, and reports back in that thread. When it joins a channel it reads your website and offers a first piece of work. It keeps an eye on what your team is discussing and brings you the next move when it spots one. (You give the OK in a quick reply, of course. Or let Kite keep going.) See **[Approvals and autonomy](/slack/approvals)**.

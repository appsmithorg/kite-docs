# Publishing Your Site

Publishing is how you take the site you have built with Kite and make it live on the internet. Until you publish, your work exists as a draft in the editor.

When you publish, Kite deploys your latest changes to a free `<name>.kite.space` web address that anyone can visit. Publishing is also a prerequisite for connecting your own custom domain. When Kite ships a site change from Slack on your say-so, it runs the same publish for you.

:::tip
You can publish as many times as you like. Each publish pushes your most recent edits live, and it takes about 40 seconds.
:::
